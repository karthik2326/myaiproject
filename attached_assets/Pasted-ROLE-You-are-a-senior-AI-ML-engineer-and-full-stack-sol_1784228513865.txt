CONTENT (Project Background):
I am building "AI-Based Real-Time Crop Image Analytics for Crop Insurance Claim Assessment under PMFBY."
Core idea: A farmer captures a photo of their damaged crop via a mobile/web app. A custom-trained CNN analyzes the image in real time to estimate:
(a) crop type
(b) cause of damage (drought, flood, pest/disease, hailstorm, lodging, fire, wildlife damage)
(c) approximate severity of damage as a percentage
This produces an automated preliminary claim report, which agriculture/insurance field staff verify before the farmer's payout is approved.
Training data comes from field staff (agriculture extension officers, PMFBY-empanelled surveyors, KVK staff) who capture geo-tagged, labeled damage images during real visits, supplemented over time by verified farmer-submitted images.

SOP (Standard Operating Procedure / Workflow to Follow):
1. Farmer captures crop image (GPS + timestamp auto-tagged) via app.
2. Image passes quality check (blur/lighting/resolution).
3. Custom CNN predicts damage type + severity %.
4. System cross-checks prediction against weather data, land records, and historical claims for anomalies.
5. Auto-generated claim report is created (crop type, damage cause, severity %, confidence score, images, GPS).
6. Field staff review the report — mandatory manual verification if confidence is low or damage value is high.
7. Approved report is forwarded to the PMFBY/insurer system for payout.
Follow this SOP as the backbone when designing any component below.

OWN NEURAL NETWORK (Requirement — Not a Black-Box API):
Design a custom CNN, built and trained on our own labeled field dataset — not a wrapped third-party vision API. Transfer learning (e.g., fine-tuning a ResNet/EfficientNet backbone) is acceptable only as a bootstrap step while our dataset is still small, with a clear path to a fully custom-trained network as more field data accumulates each season. The network should have multi-task output heads:
- Head 1: damage-type classification (softmax)
- Head 2: severity estimation (regression 0–100%, or ordinal severity buckets)
- Head 3 (optional): segmentation mask of damaged vs. healthy crop area, to compute damage % from pixel ratio
Specify the layer architecture, loss functions (cross-entropy + MSE/MAE + Dice if segmentation is used), and augmentation strategy for variable field lighting/angles.
